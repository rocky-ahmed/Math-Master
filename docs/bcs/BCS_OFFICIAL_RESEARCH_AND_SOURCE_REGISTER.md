## 2. Detailed Profile Rules: Official BPSC Rules vs. App-Configurable Settings

### 2.1 Profile A: `BCS_50TH_PRELIM` (50th BCS Preliminary — Default Active Profile)
- **Officially Confirmed Rules**:
  - **Mathematical Reasoning (গাণিতিক যুক্তি)**: **20 Marks** (5 Official Units × 4 Marks per unit).
  - **Mental Ability (মানসিক দক্ষতা)**: **15 Marks** (6 Official Categories: Verbal Reasoning, Problem Solving, Spelling and Language, Mechanical Reasoning, Space Relation, Numerical Ability).
  - **Combined Share**: **35 Marks** out of the 200-mark Preliminary MCQ test (2 hours total for 200 questions).
  - **Negative Marking in Official Circulars**: `+1.0` mark per correct response; `-0.50` mark deducted per wrong response.
- **App-Configurable Practice Settings**:
  - Proportional 35-minute default timer for the 35-question subject mock (`BCS_50TH_PRELIM_35_MOCK`).
  - User-selectable negative marking in custom practice (`0.00` Off, `-0.25` Custom Practice Penalty, `-0.50` Official/Historical BCS Reference).

### 2.2 Profile B: `BCS_WRITTEN_008_MATH` (BCS Written Subject Code 008 — Mathematical Reasoning)
- **Officially Confirmed Rules**:
  - **Subject Code**: `008` (`Mathematical Reasoning / গাণিতিক যুক্তি`).
  - **Official Full Marks**: **50 Marks** (Compulsory Written Subject; no negative marking).
  - **Official Syllabus Topics (12 Topic Groups)**:
    1. Simplification of Arithmetic and Algebraic Expressions.
    2. Unitary Method, Average, Percentage, Simple and Compound Interest, LCM & GCD, Ratio and Proportion, Profit and Loss.
    3. Algebraic Formulas, Factorization of Polynomials, Linear and Quadratic Equations, Linear and Quadratic Inequalities.
    4. Systems of Linear Equations in Two or More Variables.
    5. Exponents and Logarithms.
    6. Arithmetic and Geometric Progressions (Sequences and Series).
    7. Line, Angle, Triangle and Circle Theorems, and Pythagoras Theorem.
    8. Mensuration (Area of Plane Figures and Volume/Surface Area of Solids).
    9. Cartesian Coordinate Geometry (Distance Formula and Straight Line Equations).
    10. Trigonometry (Trigonometric Ratios, Identities, and Distance & Elevation).
    11. Set Theory and Venn Diagrams.
    12. Counting Principles, Permutations, Combinations, and Elementary Probability.
- **Historical Question Paper Patterns vs. App-Configurable Practice Settings**:
  - The official BPSC Written Syllabus document specifies **Full Marks: 50** and the 12 topic areas above, **without** mandating a fixed statutory question count or "12 choose 10" rule inside the syllabus PDF itself.
  - Historically, BPSC Written Code 008 examination papers have presented 10 questions (or 12 questions with instructions to answer 10) carrying 5 marks each (`10 × 5 = 50 marks`) requiring full step-by-step narrative derivations.
  - In Math Master, `BCS_WRITTEN_008_MATH` and `BCS_WRITTEN_008_PRACTICE` use a default practice session of **10 questions** with a 4-step Written Rubric (`দেওয়া আছে`, `প্রয়োজনীয় সূত্র`, `ধাপে ধাপে সমাধান`, `চূড়ান্ত উত্তর ও শুদ্ধি পরীক্ষা`), and both question count and duration (`120 min` full profile / `60 min` practice drill) are explicitly labeled as configurable practice settings.

### 2.3 Profile C: `BCS_WRITTEN_009_MENTAL` (BCS Written Subject Code 009 — Mental Ability)
- **Officially Confirmed Rules**:
  - **Subject Code**: `009` (`Mental Ability / মানসিক দক্ষতা`).
  - **Official Full Marks**: **50 Marks** (Compulsory Written Subject).
  - **Official Syllabus Areas (6 Cognitive Domains)**:
    1. Verbal Reasoning (ভাষাগত যৌক্তিক বিচার)
    2. Abstract Reasoning (বিমূর্ত যুক্তি)
    3. Space Relations (স্থানাঙ্ক সম্পর্ক)
    4. Numerical Ability (সংখ্যাগত ক্ষমতা)
    5. Spelling and Language (বানান ও ভাষা)
    6. Mechanical Reasoning (যান্ত্রিক দক্ষতা)
- **App-Configurable Practice Settings**:
  - Default profile configuration of 50 questions (`60 min` duration) and practice drill configuration (`BCS_WRITTEN_009_PRACTICE`, default 25 questions, `30 min`), customizable by the user.

### 2.4 Profile D: `BCS_WRITTEN_REGULAR` (Combined Written Codes 008 + 009)
- **Officially Confirmed Rules**:
  - Combines compulsory Subject Code 008 (`50 Marks`) and Subject Code 009 (`50 Marks`) = **100 Total Marks**.
- **App-Configurable Practice Settings**:
  - Provides unified tracking and combined practice sessions (`defaultQuestionCount = 20`, `durationMinutes = 120`, customizable).

### 2.5 Profile E: `BCS_51ST_SPECIAL` (51st Special BCS 2026 — Health Cadre Screening)
- **Officially Confirmed Rules**:
  - **Eligibility & Cadre Scope**: Strictly restricted to **BCS (Health) Cadre** recruitment (Assistant Surgeon / Assistant Dental Surgeon). Not applicable to General or other Technical Cadres.
  - **Official Marks Breakdown**: Within the 200-mark MCQ written/screening examination (150 marks Medical/Dental technical subject + 50 marks General subjects):
    - **Mathematical Reasoning (গাণিতিক যুক্তি)**: **10 Marks**
    - **Mental Ability (মানসিক দক্ষতা)**: **10 Marks**
    - **Combined Quantitative & Analytical Share**: **20 Marks** (`10 Math + 10 Mental Ability = 20 Marks`).
  - **Negative Marking**: `+1.0` per correct answer, `-0.50` per wrong answer.
- **App-Configurable Practice Settings**:
  - `BCS_51ST_SPECIAL_20_MOCK` generates exactly 10 Mathematical Reasoning questions + 10 Mental Ability questions = 20 questions with a proportional 20-minute timer.

### 2.6 Profile F: `BCS_LEGACY_PRELIM` (Archived 35th–49th BCS Preliminary vs. 10th–34th BCS Archive)
- **Strict Historical Scoping & Versioning**:
  - **`BCS_LEGACY_PRELIM` (`id = "BCS_LEGACY_35_49"`)**: Strictly represents the **35th–49th BCS Preliminary Syllabus (2014–2024)**, which standardized the 200-mark Preliminary test with **15 Mathematical Reasoning marks (5 Units × 3 Marks) + 15 Mental Ability marks = 30 Total Marks**.
  - **Pre-35th BCS Examinations (`10th–34th BCS`, 1989–2013)**: Conducted under the earlier 100-mark General Preliminary structure prior to the 35th BCS syllabus reform. Verified historical questions from the 10th (1989), 12th (1991), 25th (2004), 26th (2005), and 32nd (2012) BCS are preserved in the **Previous Question Practice Archive (`PREVIOUS_QUESTION_PRACTICE`, spanning 10th–46th BCS)** with their exact examination numbers and years, and are never conflated with the 35th–49th syllabus mark distribution.

## 4. Five-Tier Historical Question Provenance Classification

Every question in Module 13 carries an explicit provenance classification (`BcsProvenanceTier` / `BcsVerificationStatus`):

1. **`VERIFIED_OFFICIAL_PAPER` / `VERIFIED_HISTORICAL` (33 Total Verified Past Questions: 22 Math + 11 Mental Ability)**:
   - **22 Mathematical Reasoning Questions** (`BcsPreparationRepository.verifiedPreviousBcsQuestions`): Verified across 10th, 12th, 25th, 26th, 32nd, 37th, 40th, 41st, 43rd, 44th, and 45th BCS papers.
   - **11 Mental Ability Questions** (`BcsCurriculumAndGuideRepository.mentalAbilityQuestions` with `VERIFIED_OFFICIAL_PAPER`): Verified across 37th, 38th, 39th, 40th, 41st, 42nd, 43rd, 44th, 45th, and 46th BCS papers.
2. **`RECONSTRUCTED_EVIDENCE`**: Historical BCS question with documented typographic/archive audit notes in `provenanceAuditLog` (20 audit log entries mapping every legacy ID to its verified resolution).
3. **`SECONDARY_ARCHIVE`**: Attested in secondary public service compilations with independent mathematical proof.
4. **`CONCEPT_DERIVED_PRACTICE` / `SYLLABUS_PRACTICE_MODEL` (19 Total: 18 Math + 1 Written 009 Abstract Reasoning)**: Modeled directly on official BPSC syllabus units and NCTB textbook theorems.
5. **`GENERATED_PRACTICE`**: Deterministically parameterized practice problems for speed and mastery drills.
