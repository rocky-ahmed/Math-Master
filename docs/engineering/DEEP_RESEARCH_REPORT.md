### 1.1 Bangladesh Mathematics Olympiad (BdMO)
- **Organization**: Bangladesh Mathematics Olympiad Committee (বাংলাদেশ গণিত অলিম্পিয়াড কমিটি)
- **Official URLs**:
  - Main Portal: `https://online.matholympiad.org.bd` and `https://www.matholympiad.org.bd`
  - Syllabus & Past Question Archive: `https://matholympiad.org.bd/resources`
- **Publication / Reference Period**: Annual National & Regional Olympiad syllabi and problem sets (2003–2025 editions).
- **Source Quality Note**: **Tier-1 Primary National Authority** for Bangladeshi competitive mathematics across Primary (Classes 3–5), Junior (Classes 6–8), Secondary (Classes 9–10), and Higher Secondary (Classes 11–12).
- **Verified Research & Curriculum Findings**:
  1. **Number Theory (সংখ্যাতত্ত্ব)**: Prime factorization $N = \prod p_i^{a_i}$, divisor count $d(N) = \prod (a_i + 1)$, divisor sum $\sigma(N) = \prod \frac{p_i^{a_i+1}-1}{p_i-1}$, Euler's Totient $\varphi(N) = N \prod_{p \mid N} (1 - 1/p)$, Fermat's Little Theorem $a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod p$ for prime $p \nmid a$, Legendre's formula $E_p(n!) = \sum_{k \ge 1} \lfloor n/p^k \rfloor$, and composite divisibility criteria ($2..12, 13, 15, 16, 18, 25$).
  2. **Algebra (বীজগণিত)**: Symmetric identities, quadratic discriminant $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$, Vieta's formulas ($x_1 + x_2 = -b/a$, $x_1 x_2 = c/a$), and system of linear equations $a_1 x + b_1 y = c_1, a_2 x + b_2 y = c_2$.
  3. **Geometry & Mensuration (জ্যামিতি ও পরিমিতি)**: Triangle similarity, Pythagoras's theorem, circle chord/tangent properties, cyclic quadrilaterals, and 2D/3D mensuration invariants.
  4. **Combinatorics & Probability (কম্বিনেটরিক্স ও সম্ভাব্যতা)**: Permutations $nP_r = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}$, combinations $nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$, and classical probability $P(A) = n(A)/n(S)$.

### 1.5 Peer-Reviewed Cognitive Learning Science Literature
1. **Testing Effect & Retrieval Practice**:
   - **Authors**: Henry L. Roediger III & Jeffrey D. Karpicke
   - **Publication Date**: March 2006
   - **Journal**: *Psychological Science*, Vol. 17, No. 3, pp. 249–255 (Association for Psychological Science)
   - **URL / DOI**: `https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x`
   - **Research Finding**: Immediate and delayed active retrieval tests reduce 1-week forgetting from 52% (repeated study) to 14% (retrieval practice).
   - **Source Quality Note**: Seminal peer-reviewed empirical study (>5,000 citations).
2. **Interleaved Mathematics Practice**:
   - **Authors**: Doug Rohrer & Kelli Taylor (2007); Doug Rohrer, Robert F. Dedrick, & Sandra Stershic (2015)
   - **Publication Dates**: November 2007 (*Instructional Science*, Vol. 35, pp. 481–498) and April 2015 (*Journal of Educational Psychology*, Vol. 107, No. 3, pp. 900–908, American Psychological Association)
   - **URLs / DOIs**: `https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-007-9015-8` and `https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000001`
   - **Research Finding**: Randomized classroom trial ($N=140$ middle-school mathematics students) demonstrated that interleaved practice across distinct problem types produced **72% vs. 38% accuracy** on a 30-day delayed post-test compared to blocked practice ($d = 1.05$).
   - **Source Quality Note**: High-validity randomized controlled classroom study in mathematics education.
3. **Distributed Practice & Spaced Repetition**:
   - **Authors**: Nicholas J. Cepeda, Harold Pashler, Edward Vul, John T. Wixted, & Doug Rohrer
   - **Publication Date**: May 2006
   - **Journal**: *Psychological Bulletin*, Vol. 132, No. 3, pp. 354–380 (American Psychological Association)
   - **URL / DOI**: `https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354`
   - **Research Finding**: Meta-analysis of 254 studies ($N > 14,000$) establishing that expanding review intervals ($1\text{d} \to 3\text{d} \to 7\text{d} \to 14\text{d} \to 30\text{d}$) maximize long-term retention across verbal and quantitative tasks.
   - **Source Quality Note**: Gold-standard quantitative meta-analysis.
4. **Learning from Errors & Productive Failure**:
   - **Authors**: Manu Kapur (2008); Janet Metcalfe (2017)
   - **Publication Dates**: 2008 (*Cognition and Instruction*, Vol. 26, No. 3, pp. 379–424) and January 2017 (*Annual Review of Psychology*, Vol. 68, pp. 465–489)
   - **URLs / DOIs**: `https://doi.org/10.1080/07370000802212669` and `https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-010416-044022`
   - **Research Finding**: Corrective feedback that explains *why* an erroneous procedure failed (diagnostic misconception feedback) produces significantly higher conceptual transfer than right/wrong binary feedback alone.
   - **Source Quality Note**: Peer-reviewed synthesis in *Annual Review of Psychology*.
